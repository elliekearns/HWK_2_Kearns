What is this teaching?

This is a tiny example of pattern matching. It does not know dog breeds or look at dog photos. It turns your size and hair choices into a simple description, compares that description with its stored examples, and picks the closest match.

1. Turn clues into dataThe slider becomes a size number. The selected hair button becomes a category.
2. Compare patternsHair type matters a lot; size helps choose between similar examples, such as Poodle and Labradoodle.
3. Draw a boundaryAt some point, a small slider move makes a different example become the closest one.
4. Notice the limitsAn unusual description still gets a guess because the model must choose from its limited list.

That is why the “stretchy guess” meter matters: a confident-looking answer is not proof that the model understands dogs. Real classifiers need many examples, better inputs, and careful testing for mistakes and unfair patterns.
